Food & Beverage Market Analysis Dashboard
-----------------------------------------
----------------------------------------

==> Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing consumer preferences and market trends in the food and beverage industry using survey data collected from customers across multiple Indian cities.

The objective was to understand customer purchasing behavior, preferred sales channels, pricing expectations, and brand preferences to help businesses make data-driven decisions regarding product positioning, distribution, and marketing strategies.


--> Business Problem

The food and beverage market is highly competitive, making it important for companies to understand:

* How customers make purchasing decisions
* Which sales channels drive the most purchases
* Preferred price ranges among consumers
* Brand awareness and market perception
* Opportunities for growth in different regions

The goal of this analysis was to convert customer survey responses into actionable business insights.


--> Dataset Information

The dataset contains survey responses from over 10,000 consumers across 10 major Indian cities.

Key areas covered include:

* Consumer demographics
* Purchase behavior
* Preferred beverage brands
* Consumption frequency
* Sales channel preferences
* Price sensitivity
* Product expectations


--> Tools Used

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Excel


--> Data Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query.

Steps included:

* Handling missing values
* Standardizing categorical data
* Validating survey responses
* Creating calculated columns where required
* Preparing the data model for reporting


----------- Dashboard Features--------------

--> Market Overview

Provides a high-level view of:

* Total survey responses
* City-wise distribution
* Consumer demographics
* Overall market trends

==> Consumer Behavior Analysis

Tracks:

* Beverage consumption habits
* Purchase frequency
* Preferred product categories
* Factors influencing buying decisions

--> Sales Channel Analysis

Evaluates performance of different channels including:

* Supermarkets
* Online Retailers
* Local Stores
* Other distribution channels

The analysis helps identify where customers prefer to purchase food and beverage products.

--> Pricing Analysis

Examines:

* Consumer price expectations
* Preferred price ranges
* Pricing trends across customer groups

This helps businesses understand price sensitivity and positioning opportunities.

--> Brand Analysis

Compares leading beverage brands based on:

* Customer awareness
* Preference levels
* Market perception

The dashboard highlights areas where brands can improve customer engagement and product offerings.


--> Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

* Supermarkets emerged as the most preferred purchasing channel among consumers.
* Online retail channels showed strong adoption and represented a significant share of purchases.
* The ₹50–₹99 price range was the most preferred pricing segment among respondents.
* Customer preferences varied across cities, indicating opportunities for location-specific strategies.
* Brand comparison revealed opportunities for differentiation through product innovation and health-focused offerings.
